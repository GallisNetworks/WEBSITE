“My business needs a website.”

A straightforward website can explain your business, present what you offer and give customers a way to enquire. We’ll agree the pages and features before work begins.

02

“My current website needs work.”

Outdated information, an awkward layout or a page that needs tidying? Share the website and the changes you want. Feasibility depends on the platform and access available.

03

“I have a small business task.”

Describe the work you need help with. We’ll confirm whether it fits the available skills, can be done remotely and is small enough to take on.

Work is scheduled during evenings and weekends, by arrangement. Scope and timing are agreed for each project. This is a project service, not an emergency support desk.
