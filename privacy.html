Privacy & cookies

Review preview: the enquiry form is inactive and does not submit your details. This notice must be completed with the selected providers and business contact details before enquiries open.

Visiting this website

This redesign does not set analytics or advertising cookies, use browser storage, load external fonts or load social feeds automatically. Images and the main site assets are served with the website.

The live website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider processes technical information when serving pages. See GitHub’s privacy statement for its practices.

Social profiles and optional embeds

The website links to Gallis Networks on TikTok, Instagram and Facebook. Visiting those profiles opens the relevant platform, whose privacy practices apply.

Social content is not loaded until you select a feed button. Loading TikTok or Facebook content connects your browser to that platform, which may receive your IP address and browser information and use cookies or similar technologies. You can remove a loaded feed using its button; this stops further loading here but does not delete cookies already set by the provider. No preference is saved by this website.

Instagram is currently a direct profile link. An automatically updated Instagram feed has not been connected.

Enquiries

The form asks for your name, email, optional phone number and postcode, business or project type, service required and project details. Its intended purpose is to allow Gallis Networks to respond to your enquiry and discuss your project. Please do not include passwords or sensitive security information.

Formspree is the proposed delivery provider. It has not been connected. Cloudflare Turnstile is the proposed anti-spam check; once enabled, it will process technical browser and connection information to assess automated traffic. No challenge loads while the form is inactive. When the form is enabled, submissions will be sent to the configured form provider for delivery to the business mailbox.

Before this notice is published

The business must confirm its privacy contact, lawful basis, retention period, selected form and email providers, any international transfers and the arrangements for exercising data protection rights. The form remains disabled until the final notice has been reviewed and the delivery configuration completed.
