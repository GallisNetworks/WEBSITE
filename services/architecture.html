08 / SOLUTIONS ARCHITECTURE

Make the individual parts
work as one.

A good technology decision looks beyond a single device or platform. Bring the requirements, dependencies and next steps into one practical plan.

Understand the requirement
Define what the system needs to achieve, who uses it and which constraints matter.
Connect the pieces
Consider networks, security, devices and digital systems together rather than in isolation.
Plan the next step
Compare options and identify the priorities, dependencies and delivery arrangements for the project.

Bring the requirements into one plan

When systems depend on one another, changing one part can affect the rest. Explain the business outcome, existing equipment or platforms and the constraints you face so the dependencies and next steps can be discussed together.

Scope, availability, costs and timing are agreed before work begins.
