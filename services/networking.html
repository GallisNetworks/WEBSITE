Planning a farm or property network

Start with the areas that need a connection: the farmhouse, office, workshop or outbuildings. Describe the internet connection you already use, where coverage stops and how many devices depend on it. That context helps identify what needs further investigation.

Scope, availability, costs and timing are agreed before work begins.
