Satellite broadband and local Wi-Fi solve different parts of a connectivity problem. Discuss both the internet connection and how you want to distribute it around your property. Equipment selection, setup and any installation work must be agreed for the particular site.

Scope, availability, costs and timing are agreed before work begins.
